Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Our commitment: Your conversations and reflections are deeply personal. We treat your privacy with the utmost respect and never share, sell, or monetize your personal data.

What We Collect

To provide you with a personalized clarity experience, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address and name when you create an account
  • Conversation Data: The sessions you have with Orivya, including your messages and the insights generated
  • Library Items: Notes, saved insights, and decisions you choose to keep
  • Usage Patterns: How you interact with Orivya to improve the experience

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide personalized clarity and pattern recognition
  • Remember context across conversations
  • Generate insights based on themes in your reflections
  • Improve the overall Orivya experience

We Never

  • Sell your personal data to third parties
  • Use your conversations to train AI models without explicit consent
  • Share your insights or patterns with anyone
  • Display ads based on your personal reflections

Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for all conversations
  • Secure data storage with regular backups
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Two-factor authentication option for accounts

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all data we have about you
  • Export: Download your complete conversation history
  • Delete: Remove specific sessions or your entire account
  • Correct: Update any inaccurate information

Data Retention

Your conversations and insights are retained as long as you maintain an active account. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Cookies & Analytics

We use minimal, essential cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand general usage patterns (anonymized)

We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via email and in-app notification. Continued use of Orivya after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
