Using Orivya

Tap the "Start writing" button on your home screen or the plus button in the tab bar. You can write freely or choose from one of our guided prompts to get started.

Your note will automatically save as you type—no need to manually save anything.

Threads are a way to group related notes together. Think of them as ongoing conversations with yourself about a particular topic, like "career decisions" or "relationship with mom."

You can create threads manually or let Orivya suggest connections based on your writing.

Goals help you track progress on things you're working through. Unlike tasks, goals in Orivya are about gaining clarity—understanding what you want, making sense of a situation, or working through a decision.

Link notes to goals and Orivya will show you observations about your progress over time.

AI Features

Orivya uses AI to help you notice patterns in your writing that you might miss. It can suggest connections between notes, identify recurring themes, and offer gentle observations about what seems important to you.

The AI never judges or diagnoses—it simply reflects back what it notices to help you understand yourself better.
