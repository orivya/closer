## Persona 1: Maya & Jordan
**The Coast-to-Coast Professionals**

### Profile
| | Maya | Jordan |
|---|------|--------|
| Age | 29 | 31 |
| Location | San Francisco, CA | New York, NY |
| Occupation | Product Designer, tech startup | Investment Banker |
| Time Difference | PST | EST (+3 hours) |

### Relationship Context
- **How they met**: Mutual friends at a wedding in Cabo
- **Together**: 2 years
- **Visits**: Every 6-8 weeks, alternating who travels
- **Communication style**: Heavy texting, weekly video calls

### Their Story
Maya and Jordan fell hard at a destination wedding. For the first 6 months, they were in the same city (both in NYC), but Maya got her dream job offer in San Francisco. They decided to try long-distance rather than break up. Now 18 months into LDR, they're exhausted but committed.

### Pain Points
1. **Schedule mismatch**: Jordan's banking hours mean 10 PM calls for Maya
2. **Effort imbalance**: Maya feels she initiates 70% of communication
3. **Quality vs. quantity**: Their calls feel like status updates, not connection
4. **Future uncertainty**: Neither wants to give up their career

### How Closer Helps
- **Timezone pill** always shows Jordan's time — Maya knows when he's likely free
- **"Thinking of You" taps** let Jordan show presence without stopping work
- **Hot Takes** creates quick playful moments in their 5-minute gaps
- **Goodnight ritual** gives them a consistent end-of-day touchpoint even when Jordan works late
- **Dream Builder** for their goal: "Live in the same city by 2027"

### Sample User Flow: Monday Morning
> 7:15 AM PST — Maya opens Closer while making coffee
> Sees: "Jordan is in New York (10:15 AM) • Last active 2h ago" (he's in meetings)
> Taps the Thinking of You heart — takes 1 second
> Answers the Daily Question: "What made you smile yesterday?" → "Your text about the meeting disaster 😂"
>
> 10:30 AM EST — Jordan checks phone between meetings
> Sees notification: "Maya is thinking of you 💭"
> Smiles, feels connected for a moment
> Opens Closer, answers the same Daily Question
> Both answers revealed when he submits
>
> 9:30 PM PST — Maya's goodnight, Jordan's midnight
> They both complete the Goodnight ritual
> Maya sends: "Dream of me. 14 more days. 💕"
> Jordan sends: "Always. Can't wait."

### Monetization Potential
- **Subscription**: Closer Pro ($14.99/month) — Both high earners, value premium features
- **Purchases**: Anniversary gift bundle, seasonal gifts
- **Annual LTV**: ~$250
